    3. Disappearing chat

    Goal: speak with good intonation. Read your lines aloud with a partner. In each round, more words disappear.

    Tip: your voice goes up at the end of a yes/no question. Press “Listen” to hear a line. Your device reads it aloud.

    4. Now you do it!

    Goal: use questions and replies in real speaking. Work in pairs and tell your partner what you did yesterday.

    Find things you both did. The pair with the most similarities wins.

    • Did you … yesterday? Yes, I did. / No, I didn’t.
    • What did you …? I … (past simple)
    • Where did you go? I went to …
    • I watched TV. So did I! / Me too!
